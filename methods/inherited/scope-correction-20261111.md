**Medical XR scope and methodology correction — 11 November 2026**

This amendment follows the user's clarification and reading of [Bringing Virtual and Augmented Reality Into Healthcare](https://colelyons.com/writing/bringing-virtual-and-augmented-reality-into-healthcare/). It supersedes the rehabilitation/planning-only restriction for the main evidence assessment. Earlier screening files remain a record of the pilot, not final exclusions under this broader question. This is a specification for the next stage; it is not a retrospective claim that the pilot followed a validated, registered protocol.

**Why only 222 were screened.** The 222 comprise 20 calibration records, 42 feasibility records and a later 160-record continuation batch. The latter allocated ten keyword-routed candidates per export year 2020–2026 per assigned domain, plus 20 coverage probes. Its size was an operational batch choice, not a power calculation, validated stopping rule or demonstration of adequate coverage. Of the 18,976 provisional publication groups, 18,754 remain unscreened. Keyword routing did not scientifically exclude them.

**What 74 means.** Seventy-four of the latest 160 advanced under the former two-domain core rules. Forty-three were included by both reviewers; the remaining 31 advanced because of uncertainty or disagreement. Eighty-six were provisionally outside that core. This is not 74 eligible papers out of 18,976, and it is not the final eligible total from all 222 screens. Some previously routed-out medical education, communication, implementation and other application sources may be central to the clarified question. The entire 222-record pilot must be reassessed consistently under the broader scope; original decisions must remain visible.

**What methodology already exists.** The pilot used written inclusion/exclusion criteria, source-preserving IDs, separate AI reviewers, recorded reasons and exact excerpts, an uncertainty route to full text, and access/date/version checks. However, the queue size and approximately 80-study ceiling were workload choices. The keyword routing and AI judgments have not been evaluated against an independent human reference set. Several boundary rules evolved during the pilot, some reason codes combine alternatives, and the process has not established how many relevant records it misses. AI agreement and verbatim excerpts demonstrate consistency/provenance, not accuracy. The correct description is a documented exploratory pilot, not a completed validated textual assessment of the field.

**Locked primary question.** Since 2020, has progress from commercial XR and AI capabilities to useful, sustainable medical applications accelerated—and what technical, staffing, financial and organizational conditions enable or constrain further acceleration?

**Subsequent capability clarification.** The user approved this wording and clarified that the principal analysis should identify the enabling technologies used in studies and trace their capabilities through clinicians/educators, teams, hospitals and wider availability. Use actual study results and dated product/specification/economic evidence. A new experiment testing generic adoption conditions is not required. Capability lineages may cross products, organizations and commercial uses. Commercial performance, inherited technical capability and medical usefulness are separate outcomes. Targeted calculations or simulation are optional. See Medical-XR-Locked-Question-and-Capability-Analysis.md and the revised master proposal. COVID and the other influences remain explanatory context.

The paper should retain the broader medical landscape while giving particular attention to the article's connected uses: anatomy and procedural education; virtual-patient communication and encounter training; patient-specific surgical preparation and guidance; and XR-supported rehearsal, mentoring and robotics. Rehabilitation remains relevant but is no longer one of two gatekeeping domains. Actual remote instrument control is a distinct application and must not be inferred from simulation or telementoring.

**Design for the next stage.** Use a systematic evidence map with bibliometric and structured content analysis, followed by bounded SoS/economic case analysis. The review component can use JBI scoping-review guidance and PRISMA-ScR reporting; the additional longitudinal and SoS analyses require their own prespecified methods. PRISMA-ScR is a reporting guideline, not an automatic text classifier or evidence that a review is accurate. JBI recommends a piloted extraction form aligned with the question. [JBI methodological guidance](https://doi.org/10.11124/JBIES-20-00167); [PRISMA-ScR](https://www.prisma-statement.org/scoping).

**Three separate operations**

| Operation | Unit and coverage | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Broad eligibility screening | Every reconciled, potentially in-window publication record | Does it substantively concern XR in healthcare or health-professional education? Missing/ambiguous information advances for resolution. No two-domain restriction. |
| Structured textual coding | Eligible records, with the text level recorded | Apply defined categories to explicit source evidence; do not infer a function, deployment or cause merely from a keyword. Abstract-derived and full-text-derived results remain distinguishable. |
| Deeper full-text/system analysis | A selected subset from the eligible frame, plus explicit explanatory cases | Determine allocation across years and use cases before selection. Keep purposive cases separate from estimates of field prevalence. The previous 80 is not a required sample size. |

Reviews, protocols, conceptual reports and empirical studies receive separate evidence-type tags. They may inform the research landscape but cannot be pooled as independent empirical studies. News and vendor/financial documents belong in a separate commercial/context ledger. An ordinary video visit, text-only chatbot or isolated AI model is not automatically XR; related non-XR evidence can explain context without inflating the XR count. Preserve original-query Web of Science comparisons separately from any merged, medically screened multi-database map.

**Minimum coding specification to pilot and version**

| Field | Decision rule |
|---|---|
| Use case | Multiple labels allowed: anatomy/procedural training; patient communication/virtual patients; patient-specific planning; intraoperative guidance; team rehearsal/mentoring/robotics; rehabilitation; other specified medical application; unclear. Record the stated purpose. Overlapping categories do not sum to unique studies. |
| Technology | Identify VR, AR, MR, or related interactive 3D; display/interface and platform/version when reported. Keep adjacent non-XR systems separately tagged. |
| Evidence type and maturity | Record what was actually evaluated: concept/protocol, technical performance, usability/feasibility, learning or patient outcome, observed service use, or continued use. These are distinct claims, not an assumed progression score. |
| AI | Record function and status separately: mention, proposed, implemented or evaluated. Distinguish dialogue/feedback, image or model preparation, guidance/control, and offline research analysis. Missing information is unknown. |
| Commercial relationship | Named supplier/device/software; actual capability used; funding, ownership and support relationship when evidenced. No Meta-only eligibility requirement. |
| COVID and other influences | Distinguish an author's stated motivation/context, an observed change in delivery/workflow, a measured association, and analyst interpretation. A COVID mention alone is not an effect. |
| Resources and economics | Capture quantities, staff roles/time, prices and currency/year, subsidies, maintenance/support, perspective and measured versus assumed costs. Missing prices or AI details never cause exclusion. |
| Time and provenance | Publication date, study/data-collection period, known report versions, source database and coverage. Record passage/page, reviewer, decision and codebook version for consequential codes. |

Human-verified development examples should include education and communication records previously excluded, ambiguous XR terminology, non-headset systems, negative/null findings, and sparse abstracts. Validate on a separate, held-out sample drawn across years, sources, included/uncertain/excluded routing and keyword misses. Specify its size and acceptance criteria before measuring performance. Report missed relevant records and class-specific errors, not only agreement. Until this exists, describe AI coding as provisional and do not claim a validated automated exclusion process. Standardize reason codes and document adjudication; preserve earlier versions when rules change.

**What analysis can establish.** Assess changes in publication volume, use-case mix, modalities, implemented AI functions, evidence types, reported medical use and resources. Use explicit denominators and comparable database/query coverage. Establish any claimed commercial investment surge from primary financial sources, distinguishing investment, spending, operating losses and other categories. Relate dated capabilities and reported influences to medical findings, accounting for study-versus-publication timing and plausible delays. Classify connections as documented, associated, hypothesized or unknown.

The June 2020 start supports analysis during and after the pandemic period; it cannot by itself identify the effect of the pandemic's onset. Historical reviews supply context without a new pre-2020 primary corpus. Simultaneous changes in investment, COVID, AI and publishing do not establish their separate causal effects. Publication growth is research activity, not clinical adoption. Cross-application clinical effect pooling is not planned; any later meta-analysis needs a suitably comparable question and evidence subset.

**Immediate consequences.** Preserve the completed work as pilot data; reopen the former domain-based exclusions; resolve the broad screening/coding rules before scaling; and process the complete eligible-date inventory rather than stopping at another arbitrary batch. Establish actual eligible use-case coverage before selecting the deeper sample. No new records were screened as part of this clarification, and no current finding should be relabeled as the result of the amended method.
