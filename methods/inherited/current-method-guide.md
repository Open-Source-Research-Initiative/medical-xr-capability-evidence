The immutable [method 0.4.6](method-v0.4.6.md) preserves the original 0.3 heading and chronological amendments. Read later amendments as superseding earlier conflicting text. Earlier wording remains an audit trail, not a competing current rule.

- Lines127 onward, **0.4.4**: record reported evidence/design type, source basis and checked status for every assessed ID. UNCLEAR after a source check differs from a pending check.
- Lines115 onward, **0.4.3**: audit modality and evidence-stream disagreements as well as eligibility; unsupported display classifications stay uncertain.
- Lines97 onward, **0.4.2**: a substantive medical/prevention task is required. Generic exercise or mood/physiological outcomes alone do not establish it; healthy samples are not excluded automatically.
- Lines81–96, metadata corrections: provisional publication groups do not establish unique studies. Family disposition, review role and unverified components remain UNASSESSED when not source-checked.
- Lines53 onward, **0.4**: context/review routing, missing information, report versions and selective full-text access remain distinct. INCLUDE at title/abstract means advance for full-text assessment, not final inclusion.
- Lines140–143, **0.4.5**: encoding repair only; older invalid-byte method files remain history.
- [Integrity addendum 0.4.6-01](integrity-routing-addendum-v0.4.6-01.md): retain retracted/concerned report IDs and notices separately, flag integrity uncertainty, withhold from synthesis pending review; do not infer fabrication or automatically exclude ordinary corrections.
