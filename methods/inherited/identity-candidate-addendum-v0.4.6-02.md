# Identity candidate addendum 0.4.6-02

The original 18,976 provisional publication IDs remain the complete screening denominator. Every ID requires both passes even when it shares DOI text, title, authors, registration or reported results with another group.

The repeated-DOI locator includes the representative DOI and all preserved `dois_json` values, using whitespace stripping and casefolding only. It identifies232 repeated DOI strings across476 IDs. This is a source-identity verification queue, not an adjudicated duplicate list. Shared conference/proceedings DOI values, source errors, versions and linked reports can produce matches. Verify title, authors, article identifiers, report type, dates and study/cohort details before assigning relationships. Preserve uncertain relationships and distinguish publication/report identity from underlying study identity.

The all-ID ledger links candidates with PENDING_SOURCE_VERIFICATION. Absence from this locator does not establish uniqueness. Original title/author/year candidate links also remain available and may find relationships with distinct DOIs. Keep the narrower representative-only locator as versioned history.

Do not transfer screening decisions or integrity labels automatically between matched IDs. Preserve each independent assessment and withhold the other reviewer's decisions before its corresponding batch is complete. A confirmed integrity notice can be linked as source evidence, with affected identities verified separately; a notice absent from the frozen inventory is an external linked source and does not silently change the denominator. Human identity verification remains pending.
