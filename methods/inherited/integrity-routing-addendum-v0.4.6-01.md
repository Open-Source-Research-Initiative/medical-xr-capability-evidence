# Integrity routing addendum 0.4.6-01

Operational addendum to screening method0.4.6; the locked question and medical-task eligibility scope are unchanged.

When a record is labeled retracted or carries an unresolved expression of concern, retain its original publication ID and source memberships. Record the integrity notice as a separate linked source, using its DOI/URL and date. Do not invent an inventory ID for a notice absent from the frozen inventory.

At this provisional AI stage, route the affected record as UNCERTAIN with an explicit integrity note and WITHHOLD_FROM_SYNTHESIS until source and human review determine final disposition. This is not ordinary empirical inclusion and is distinct from a retrieval gap. Keep the report available for historical/context and audit purposes. Verify the notice before characterizing the reason; do not equate ethical-approval concerns, editorial decisions or retraction alone with fabricated data. Preserve any stated author disagreement without resolving it on the authors' or publisher's behalf.

An ordinary correction is not automatically a retraction or an exclusion. Identify the corrected content and retain original/corrected versions separately. If effect values or participant denominators remain contradictory, withhold those unresolved values from synthesis while retaining the source-level eligibility decision where appropriate.

All decisions remain provisional AI assessments. Model agreement is not human verification. Original independent decisions must remain unchanged; post-notice rechecks are appended and explicitly source-enriched.
