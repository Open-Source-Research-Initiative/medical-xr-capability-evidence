2. The new empirical queue covers original observed or experimentally generated evidence using XR or interactive **or patient-specific** 3D centrally in an evaluated medical task. Broad tasks include anatomy/procedural education, patient/clinician communication, surgery preparation/planning/guidance, rehearsal/mentoring/robotics, rehabilitation, substantive prevention, healthcare workflow/implementation and other medical tasks. Phantom, healthy-volunteer, technical validation, usability, qualitative, implementation and economic evidence can qualify for their stated claims. Static patient-specific 3D is not automatically excluded. No requirement for AI, commercial hardware, costs, comparator, positive findings, sample size, English, open access or full text. Do not exclude solely for nonhuman test objects/veterinary scope when medical/public-health relevance could apply.
3. Eligibility is `INCLUDE` (advance for full-text assessment), `EXCLUDE` (from new empirical queue, retain appropriate context) or `UNCERTAIN`. Reasons: `ELIGIBLE`, `DATE`, `MEDICAL_TASK`, `TECHNOLOGY`, `EVIDENCE_TYPE`, `INSUFFICIENT_DESCRIPTION`. Missing substantive text stays `UNCERTAIN` with access pending, even when the title independently suggests a review/protocol/context route. Never use access failure as exclusion. A blank abstract is a retrieval locator, not proof of unavailable full text. A sufficient abstract can support advancement despite missing full text. Record assessed extent honestly.
4. Evidence streams: `empirical`, `review_context`, `protocol`, `commercial_context`, `adjacent_context`, `uncertain`. Reviews, protocol-only reports, commercial material and context are ordinarily `EXCLUDE/EVIDENCE_TYPE` from the empirical queue while retained in their stream. A protocol-labelled report with actual observed relevant technical, usability, implementation or other evaluation can advance; planned testing/enrollment/prototype alone is not observed evaluation. Real interviews after conceptual videos demonstrate attitudes, not implemented XR use: retain uncertainty where medical organizational relevance is unresolved. Generic opinion/hypothetical systems without implementation/observations belong in context/protocol. Keep actual, planned and contextual use separate.
5. A source-supported substantive clinical, rehabilitation, targeted prevention/health-service, medical education or other medical task is required. Generic exercise, ordinary leisure/tourism, or physiological/mood measures alone do not establish it. Healthy participants are eligible when the task is medical. Explicit rehabilitation, therapeutic activities, disability assistive evaluation and clinical wellbeing are not excluded for using games/exercise/recreation. Unresolved linkage is `UNCERTAIN/MEDICAL_TASK`; do not invent a blanket wellness exclusion.
6. Modality: `XR`, `nonimmersive_3D`, `uncertain`. Unspecified VR/AR wording, a generic introduction about immersion, subjective presence ratings, hardware ownership or tracking/streaming use do not alone establish delivered spatial XR. **Task-specific Methods/intervention wording describing an actual immersive VR intervention supports provisional XR even without a named device.** Explicit immersive planned interventions can be XR/protocol, labelled planned. Nonimmersive alone does not establish 3D. Interactive or patient-specific 3D can qualify separately; ordinary 2D, incidental images, generic rendering without evaluated medical spatial task and pure AI enter adjacent context. If eligible technology is supported but XR versus nonimmersive 3D remains unclear, INCLUDE with uncertain modality is possible. If whether any eligible spatial technology was evaluated is unclear, keep eligibility uncertain. Mixed empirical reports can have XR report-level with separately supported components; do not assume all observations used XR or count an ID twice. Mixed reviews remain modality uncertain when their mix is not established.
