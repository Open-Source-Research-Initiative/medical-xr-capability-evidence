# Reading the screening ledgers

These are provisional publication-ID assessments under screening method0.4.6/addenda01–04 and capability codebook0.2. This note explains administrative fields; it changes no eligibility rule or scientific category.

Queue memberships overlap. Counts of initial judgments, publication IDs, source rechecks, audit obligations and source reports must not be added as if they were distinct studies. The manifest defines the exact published snapshot; additional immutable worker submissions enter the next validated refresh.

An empirical report can contain observed design, technical, needs, measurement-development or implementation data about XR without observing delivery of an XR intervention. Inspect source extent, evidence type and modality components before deriving any observed-use denominator; an XR target/category is not proof of clinical use. Incidental-exposure rows identify whether the actor was assigned an initial or a legacy source recheck; they do not transfer authorship of a legacy initial to the fresh worker.

### Preserved stream vocabulary variants

Some current legacy/early source adjudications retain historical_context, protocol_context, adjacent or bibliometric_context tokens. They remain verbatim with the source-authored rationale; these unharmonized map-role labels are not additional observed-intervention categories. Fresh Sol explicitly declared adjacent a literal adjacent_context spelling alias in the hashed source-audit0062-readiness-application-clarification.json, without changing routing or rewriting originals. Its eight earlier organizational_readiness rows were individually source-reconciled to empirical in0062; two advanced, one exclusion became uncertain, five stayed uncertain. Prospective fresh outputs use the six current method stream names. Actual observations, medical/spatial applicability, exposure/delivery and extraction depth remain separate. Original-only agreement is not recomputed from these adjudicated field clarifications.
