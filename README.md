# Medical XR: from capabilities to sustainable use

Extended reality (XR) includes virtual, augmented and mixed reality. This project asks how those capabilities—and related interactive or patient-specific 3D and AI—become useful, sustainable medical applications.

The intended contribution is a traceable map connecting technologies, medical tasks, outcomes and the staffing, financial and organizational conditions behind adoption. It is for researchers, clinicians, engineers and health-system teams assessing what works, for whom and under what conditions.

> Since 2020, has progress from commercial XR and AI capabilities to useful, sustainable medical applications accelerated—and what technical, staffing, financial and organizational conditions enable or constrain further acceleration?

This question is locked. The work synthesizes published evidence and technology histories; it recruits no patients and delivers no care.

## Evidence and limits

The corpus groups reports provisionally; it is not 18,976 independent studies. It includes broad medical tasks, technical and implementation observations, and separate context streams. INCLUDE means advance for later full-text assessment.

The follow-up search queries, dates and filters were not recovered from the saved exports. Missing sources, dates, report families and sustainability follow-up remain unresolved. Shared model errors are possible. Publication volume and agent agreement do not answer the research question.

The complete original workspace is available as a [versioned evidence release](data/README.md#complete-evidence-archive), with a [complete file manifest](provenance/legacy-file-manifest.csv) and [publication/rights record](provenance/open-data-publication.json). Original Git history remains preserved through the [hash-linked history map](provenance/history-map.json); two historical draft commits remain unchanged outside public Git history. The public tree contains 51 unchanged historical Python copies. The archive includes source records cleared by Cole on 15 November 2026; unrelated private context and original controlled Git bundles are outside this release.
