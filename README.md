# Medical XR: from capabilities to sustainable use

Extended reality (XR) includes virtual, augmented and mixed reality. This project asks how those capabilities—and related interactive or patient-specific 3D and AI—become useful, sustainable medical applications.

The intended contribution is a traceable map connecting technologies, medical tasks, outcomes and the staffing, financial and organizational conditions behind adoption. It is for researchers, clinicians, engineers and health-system teams assessing what works, for whom and under what conditions.

> Since 2020, has progress from commercial XR and AI capabilities to useful, sustainable medical applications accelerated—and what technical, staffing, financial and organizational conditions enable or constrain further acceleration?

This question is locked. The work synthesizes published evidence and technology histories; it recruits no patients and delivers no care.

**Status: provisional research; screening paused; no acceleration finding or documented human-verified records.** Both initial assessments cover all 18,976 provisional publication groups. Source adjudication is incomplete. [Exact checkpoint and pending queues](docs/checkpoint.md).
