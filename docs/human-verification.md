The [proposed human validation design](human-validation-plan.md) now details stage-matched references, reviewer expertise, probability frames, separate exclusion/advancement endpoints, sample-size choices, missing-source accounting and source/decision packets. It is a plan for human approval, not completed validation. The [feasibility milestone](feasibility-milestone.md) specifies a small worked evidence chain before a wider acceleration analysis.

It is a proposal, not a completed or probability-based accuracy study. Freeze IDs and the frame hash before human outcomes. Hide AI decisions in source-only packets until independent human entries are saved; mark unavoidable prior exposure.

Report denominators separately: attempted, source accessed, complete required extent, verified, unresolved and discrepant. Describe missed passages, mistaken date/version identity, unsupported modality, shared model errors and disagreements about scope. Compare false exclusion and false inclusion separately where an appropriate reference assessment exists. Do not infer an accuracy rate from purposive examples or treat model agreement as ground truth.

A later probability-based validation needs a prespecified frame, uncertainty/precision plan, justified sample size and independent methods/domain input. Missing evidence outside the inventory needs an independent search or sentinel-paper assessment; it cannot be measured from this corpus alone. A human result never silently overwrites initials: corrections need attributed, ordered evidence records and separate authorization to resume scientific work.
